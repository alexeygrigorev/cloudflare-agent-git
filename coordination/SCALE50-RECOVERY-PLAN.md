## At a missed checkpoint

Diagnose the failing transition. Execute bounded repair, supported workaround or received healthy-owner handoff through existing principals/heads. Verify resumed work; if it still fails, retain the original miss and exact pending state. Change an ineffective remedy instead of repeating a reminder. Independent useful work continues between desktop checks.

Record: task and target/actual/as-of/coverage; current owner ACK; confirmed cause versus hypothesis; steps already taken and actual result; verifier/resumed action; next action/owner/dependency/due; durable trigger. A blocker-only list, proposed fix, delivery receipt or passing source test is insufficient.

Use existing launcher/supervisor and accepted role/resource rules. No duplicate batch/service/monitor/writer, arbitrary provider split, fake work, old count promoted to current, busy/draft injection or readiness/quota bypass. The scale coordination helper is GPT-6 Luna / MAX, not Sol, and is not counted as a useful worker.

Keep the existing desktop 30-minute automation active in its current chat; separate standup/publication schedules retain custody. Notify meaningful recovery, results and missed promised checkpoints with concrete action; keep other unchanged non-actionable state quiet. Do not withdraw oversight until the relevant accepted handoff/autonomy conditions are proved.

Historical analysis and provenance, including differing supported-read evidence among analysts, remain in the original version in Git history. They do not establish revised-input consensus or current runtime acceptance.
