## Responsibility and promise

The root owns follow-through from human intake through a result shown to the human. The principal owns continuous operational recovery and heads own execution, independent review and integration. Root monitoring means verifying obligations and correcting failed custody; it does not mean doing every product task or merely forwarding reports. When an owner fails to act, the root must initiate bounded diagnosis/recovery through current genuine owners and verify received custody and resumed action. Silence never closes a request.

Promise durable tracking, owned action, recovery and honest delivery. Do not promise unconditional technical success. Actual missing authority, exhausted eligible providers or a consequential human tradeoff remain explicit dependencies. Continue useful independent work, preserve the request and prepare the concrete decision before asking the human. Routine authorized diagnosis, repair and safe handoff need no new permission.

## Intake contract

Capture the exact human request and known source/time in the same turn; unknown time stays unknown. Assign a stable request ID and link it to existing canonical task IDs, rather than create duplicate tasks. Preserve the original ask, subsequent steering and explicit supersession separately. The request contains:

- Desired observable outcome, acceptance criteria, scope, authority and dependencies.
- Genuine primary owner and recovery owner, native identity/host/parent and current epoch; received scope ACK and source receipt.
- Exact workspace/owned paths, integration owner, source pin, independent reviewer requirement and fallback.
- Owner-ACK, first-action, progress, review, outcome and delivery checkpoints, each with UTC occurrence/due time and Berlin display.
- Evidence references and coverage; next executable action and durable continuation trigger.

An intake is not fully owned until a current recipient semantically accepts scope, next action and checkpoint. The intake owner retains the obligation until that ACK. A delivery receipt or old owner's ACK cannot transfer it.

## Transition rules

| State/event | Evidence required before transition | Automatic obligation on a miss |
|---|---|---|
| Recorded → owned | Genuine current owner scope ACK, epoch and checkpoint | Intake/root recovery owner diagnoses reachability/readiness, seeks safe current custody, preserves pending envelope |
| Owned → executing | Actual operation/tool receipt for documentation, browser assistance or deterministic work; delegated model execution additionally requires maintained launcher receipt and real model first action bound to task, invocation and generation | Owner diagnoses route/config/quota/workspace; repairs or hands off to a verified alternative; records actual result |
| Executing → review pending | Actual terminal result and immutable artifact/source digest; failed attempts remain failed | Owner bounds stalled work, preserves outputs and reconciles uncertain side effects before retry |
| Review pending → accepted | Distinct reviewer identity/first action, pinned verdict and evidence matching the task's semantic criteria; owning head accepts | Review owner receives obligation and due trigger; negative review reopens owned repair, never silently completes |
| Accepted → integrated/delivered | Required integration or deployment and verification at the stated evidence level; root shows usable result | Delivery owner retains an outstanding obligation; source acceptance alone cannot close deployment/publication scope |
| Completion → next useful action | Committed continuation obligation, executable owned READY contract, fresh admission and next model first action | Failed refill becomes a persistent owned repair; accepted predecessor remains accepted while continuation failure stays open |

Every transition records request/task/attempt IDs, actor, host, parent, generation, epoch, occurred time, evidence digests and source clock. Reject stale-owner writes, invalid predecessors and missing evidence. Reopen/cancel/supersede events preserve history and reasons. Close the human request only when all acceptance criteria and human delivery are met, or the human explicitly cancels/supersedes it.

## Deadlines and recovery

Proposed initial service objectives for principal/head adoption: capture same turn, owner ACK within five minutes, first action within ten minutes, useful progress checkpoint within fifteen minutes unless an explicit task-specific computation contract justifies longer. These are proposals, not human-imposed deadlines or installed timers. Adoption records the owner, actual due timestamp, recovery consequence and measurement coverage. Existing accepted task deadlines take precedence; no silently extending a missed promise.

Required implementation: the maintained supervisor must check transition obligations on completion/failure/dependency events and bounded due scans. Full production enforcement is not yet proved. The root's existing thirty-minute check audits outcomes and exercises bounded recovery when custody fails; it is not the dispatch clock. A due scan must not wait for the human or desktop heartbeat.

At every miss, record confirmed cause versus hypothesis, diagnosis/repair/workaround/handoff already executed, actual result, verification and resumed work, named next owner/action/dependency/due and durable trigger. A promised fix or blocker-only list is insufficient. Retry only an operation with supported idempotency or reconciled effects. Bound attempts by the task contract and provider health; recurring failure changes route or recovery ownership rather than repeat the same reminder.

If a principal/head misses two semantic checkpoints, apply the existing reviewed failover policy: verify actual loss/readiness, exclusive current epoch, scoped successor ACK and source/cursor custody. Never infer readiness from absent state, assume quota is healthy, bypass a protected draft, inject a busy pane or launch a duplicate writer. A paused former owner must be fenced at the write/dispatch target. Ambiguous send/write intent without a receipt is reconciled before retry. No automatic takeover of protected source leases.

## Multi-host work and delivery

Win35 root monitors and orchestrates Hetzner plus useful local work through the same contracts. Host/session/generation attribution and shared provider reservations must deduplicate actual actors. Physical secure outbound AgentBus task → model result/reply → independent review → next first action, plus offline/reconnect dedup and cursor reconciliation, is the acceptance goal. SSH is an authorized bootstrap/recovery baseline; it does not establish the requested native communication product. A placeholder URL, cloned source or poll with zero tasks does not establish useful cross-host execution.

For reports, preserve existing remote writer/publication ownership, genuine Claude Opus, full stylint and independent review. Starting after 6 October, require fresh ImageGen generation evidence and owner acceptance/integration. The root proactively delivers the verified published article URL and short share-ready summary once per edition/material correction. A draft, standup or GitHub source is explicitly labeled and cannot substitute for publication. No automatic social posting.

## Preserved boundaries

Use maintained launcher/supervisor/Bus and existing teams. Preserve privacy, exact identity, dirty peer work, ordinary Git recovery, independent review, quota and spending gates, containment, disk/scratch and Rust/global-install holds. The initiative RAM override prevents estimate/free-RAM admission refusal; it does not waive other safeguards. Shared provider capacity is aggregate across hosts, not a per-host allowance. No fake work or fixed provider split to reach fifty. Count current distinct useful workers/subagents with coverage, separately from heads/services/queued/completed actors.

## What the human should see

Deliver the accepted result, meaningful executed recovery or a prepared genuine decision. A missed agreed target/checkpoint also includes timestamped actual/target/coverage, concrete recovery already executed, owner/task IDs, next due and acceptance proof still needed. Avoid repeated unchanged blocker reports. Measure human reminders and manual rescues; repeated interventions are a defect assigned to the maintained path, not a request that the human supervise harder.
