## Challenge and falsification gate

The observed pain is about incompatible requirements and coordination overhead. It does not yet establish willingness to adopt a separate hosted repository or that Agent Branches outperforms ordinary worktrees plus an integrator. Internal SDK/auth smoke tests establish engineering behavior, not that product claim.

Head-owned next gate: assign an unfamiliar actor a genuine pending maintenance task with equal ordinary Git and prototype access. Record the actual job, actor's first action, un-steered adoption decision, time including troubleshooting, repair effort, result and fallback. Do not seed a conflict, require a favorable verdict or infer efficacy from a tie/no hazard. A separate source verifier should challenge these primary comments and the competitor's documented capabilities without installing it. Keep final-six and deployment approvals separate.
