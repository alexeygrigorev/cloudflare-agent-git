# Independent verification of official rules (Claude principal)

Source: https://www.cloudflare.com/documents/build-next-gen-git-platform-competition-terms.pdf (fetched 2026-10-02, pdftotext). Independently confirms Codex message C-R1-RULES.

- E-C009 Contest period: "begin on October 1, 2026 at 9:00 AM EDT ... end on October 14, 2026 at 11:59 PM PDT" (= Oct 15 08:59 CEST). Sponsor clock is authoritative.
- E-C010 Eligibility (s.3): "be a legal resident of the United States or Canada; and (2) be eighteen (18) years of age or older". Header: "VOID OUTSIDE OF THE UNITED STATES AND CANADA". -> ELIGIBILITY IS AN OPEN QUESTION FOR THE ORCHESTRATOR/USER; we do not infer residency. Research and prototyping are unaffected; no submission is authorized for us anyway.
- E-C011 Submission criteria (s.4): "must use Cloudflare's developer platform, including Cloudflare Workers and Artifacts, to build their Project, which must enable multiple agents working on changes concurrently." Licence MIT / Apache-2.0 / BSD-2/3 "and the repository must include a LICENSE file".
- E-C012 "Submissions may not contain personal attacks on anyone or any discernible product, including competitor products." -> demo video must not disparage GitHub etc.; frame positively.
- E-C013 "Persons may not enter using robotic, programmed, or any other automated means of entry" -> entry by a human; agent-built project is fine.
- E-C014 Judging (s.6), each scored 1-5: originality and quality of the prototype for agent-oriented software collaboration 50%; effectiveness of multi-agent concurrency, coordination, context preservation, review, and conflict handling 25%; ease of use and product/user experience 25%. Tie-break on originality.
- E-C015 Finalists present 10 minutes live at Cloudflare Connect, Moscone West SF, October 21, 2026; winner "must be physically present".
- One submission per entrant.

Scoring implication: originality/quality is half the score. A polished but derivative "GitHub + agents" will lose; the 25% concurrency criterion explicitly lists coordination, context preservation, review, conflict handling — our demo should visibly touch all five.
