## What to postpone

Habit loops from *Hooked*, audience programs from *Superfans*, and scaling playbooks are lower priority until people repeatedly complete a useful workflow. For developer tools, reduced friction and reliable outcomes can be more valuable than increased engagement. The monetization archive's enterprise shipping sprint is a possible future validation/delivery model; its financial inputs are assumptions, not a forecast or permission to sell/contact people.

## Challenges to our current direction

1. **More execution capacity is not necessarily more learning.** Your own operating-model notes favor a few operating loops and low coordination overhead. Keep wide exploration, but assign additional workers to discriminating tests rather than redundant research or additional layers of supervision.
2. **Dogfooding is necessary engineering feedback, but insufficient market validation.** Aplexer failures and worktree storage are real personal incidents. Establish who else has the same recurring costly problem and whether the prototype outperforms their existing workaround.
3. **We should not build a new Git platform merely because storage hurts.** The corrected host audit attributes 69.4GiB of 111.7GiB worktree physical union to dependencies/builds (62.1%). Test a small workspace diagnostic plus safe dependency sharing and lifecycle controls before assuming the solution requires remote Git. This is not permission to remove existing worktrees.
4. **Six is a selection target, not evidence.** Reuse archive mechanisms without manufacturing six distinct viable products. Keep the open slot open until a differentiated job passes its own test.

## Evidence discipline and immediate handoff

Keep one compact evidence card per active hypothesis using existing files: user/job, observed incident, current workaround, engineering result, external-use result, competitor comparison, next falsifier, owner and decision. Do not add a parallel reporting system. Distinguish observed facts, archived claims, synthetic fixtures, actual agent outcomes and recommendations.

Principals choose how to apply these recommendations and challenge them. Existing owners retain their lanes: Grok/independent ZCode on the live A01 comparison, storage investigators on real supported builds, Antigravity/Muse and the current repair executors on aplexer. This review does not launch duplicate implementation teams or imply consensus.
