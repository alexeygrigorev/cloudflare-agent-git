# AgentBus role continuity protocol

Status: normative design for independent implementation review; source tests alone do not prove deployed recovery. Human request of 6 October authorizes proactive recovery, role promotion, replacement agents and Hetzner coordination. Desktop periodic automation is paused and must stay paused here.

## Scope and guarantees

Roles attach to a project, optionally a subproject, rather than a named computer or model. The coordinator checks role health, periodic checkpoints and standups, resolves missing outcomes, executes recovery handoffs and verifies execution. The principal sets priorities and cross-team coverage. Heads own product backlog, implementers, independent reviews, integration and refill. Supervisors are deterministic persistence mechanisms; a healthy service does not prove a healthy model role. A coordinator may run on Hetzner or another enrolled user computer. No human, desktop chat, principal or head is a necessary approval gate for authorized routine recovery.

Initial implementation uses the existing Hetzner AgentBus store as one atomic authority, with separate lock-protected role records and an independently scheduled standby watcher. This removes individual-agent and desktop dependencies; it DOES NOT eliminate failure of Hetzner, its disk or the authority store. Another host may take a role only by accessing that same authority. An unreachable authority forbids election and new authority-controlled shared mutations; existing isolated useful tasks may finish and checkpoint. Never create independent writable authority copies or elect from an unauthenticated mirror. A later three-independent-host majority authority is necessary for availability after full authority-host loss; unavailable quorum cannot safely have both liveness and single ownership during a partition. This is an explicit delivery limitation, not a fabricated no-single-point-of-failure guarantee.

## Durable configuration

A project records project_id, parent_project_id, authorized scope, policy revision, check cadence, Berlin standup schedule/timezone, launcher policy and role slots. Each slot key is (project_id, subproject_id, role). Each slot records epoch, holder authentic Bus identity, actor/session generation, host, lease authority boot identity and expiry, renewal and useful-progress receipts, next checkpoint and standby candidate list. Children inherit policy constraints but NOT holder authority or write credentials. Role mutations and task dispatches use the slot key plus current epoch and holder credential. A holder change increments epoch monotonically; never reset epochs after restart or corrupt-state recovery. Corrupt authority fails closed and requires recovery from a validated checkpoint with a higher generation.

Candidates explicitly ACK role eligibility, read current governance, have enrolled identity, scoped permissions, healthy actual runtime/provider route, fresh quota eligibility and a recoverable checkpoint. Readable hostname/tag/PID is not identity. Rank eligible candidates by recorded priority then stable identity ID; incumbent does not compete while valid. Evaluate eligibility before election; skip unhealthy candidates with recorded evidence. Promotion of a head atomically assigns the principal slot and marks its former head slot replacement-required; existing child task leases remain assigned and useful jobs are preserved. Launch a replacement head or principal only through the maintained gated launcher, with an idempotent launch intent (slot, epoch, recovery incident). No guessed adapter/free model route or duplicate launch on retry.

## Health and missed checks

A lightweight watcher tick runs every 60 seconds independently of model turns. Default role lease TTL 180 seconds, renewed at most every 60 seconds using authority-clock/boot-scoped monotonic time. Lease renewal proves holder contact, not useful progress. Default semantic sync deadline 120 seconds; default expected useful action after takeover 300 seconds. Project-specific changes must be explicit configuration with reason and acceptance evidence. Root periodic reports remain every 30 minutes, daily standup 09:00 Europe/Berlin. Watchers reconcile the durable last scheduled slot/run ID and schedule grace, rather than starting a new controller on each report miss. A 120-second report grace triggers suspicion/diagnosis, not proof of death. Standup idempotency key includes project, Berlin local date and schedule revision, including DST-aware next due calculation.

Health states are healthy, suspected, diagnosed-unresponsive, lease-expired, takeover-pending, recovered, or held-by-policy. For a missed response/check: persist an incident and owner; send exact sync envelope requesting current epoch, identity, task/progress receipt and next checkpoint; inspect genuine native catalog, exact PID/start time, process/service/cgroup and recent useful tool/artifact evidence via authorized host adapter. Record timeout/transport failure/unknown distinctly. A busy model or protected draft is not dead and must not receive pane injection. Inbox delivery is the default. Read ACK, semantic sync response and useful work outcome are separate records. Work can be alive but its coordinator role unresponsive: replace coordination custody after lease expiry without killing productive implementation.

## Election and fencing

Only the authoritative transaction can select a winner, expire/revoke a slot, advance epoch and reserve replacement launch. Concurrent standbys submit the same incident id; one atomic decision wins. Observations/sync messages do not themselves grant authority. A stale holder cannot renew, hand off, assign tasks, publish role decisions or release newer leases. Clock rollback/reboot invalidates boot-scoped leases; epoch survives reboot. Recovery never declares liveness solely from stale restored TUI/history/PID.

Every mutating consumer must validate current (slot, epoch, holder) immediately before accepting its effect: task claim, dispatch reservation, role/task registry writes, canonical integration and recovery launch. Stale source decisions are rejected and audited. A module providing epochs without enforcing them at actual launcher/registry consumers is a source primitive, not end-to-end split-brain prevention. Git pushes/shared file writes that cannot be fenced must use existing owned-path locks and deny overlapping custody until prior owner is quiesced/checkpointed or writes are isolated. Do not kill an agent merely because its lease expired; fence authority and preserve productive children.

## Recovery execution

After grant, new holder reads durable project state, pending exact envelopes/read cursors, open incidents, active task leases, reviews and READY backlog. It ACKs custody under its own identity, records its first real action, reconciles already launched/completed jobs and resumes independent eligible work. Former head replacement is a separate tracked task; promotion is not a license to abandon that team. Failures produce owner, confirmed diagnosis vs hypothesis, action already executed, result, dependencies, next bounded step/deadline and independent acceptance criteria. Retry uses capped backoff and recorded attempts; provider failure selects a freshly eligible alternative. Policy hold produces an owned repair/handoff and continues unrelated work, not an unexplained end of turn.

The old holder rejoins as a non-holder, reads newer epoch, checkpoints its private output and offers a reviewed handoff. It never resumes authority from stored credentials. Graceful handoff is an atomic epoch advance after successor ACK, with exact cursor/task custody reconciliation. Replacement launch success needs genuine enrollment/first model tool; controller/PID/read ACK alone is insufficient. A startup failure leaves the slot takeover-pending and tries another eligible candidate, while preserving the failed intent receipt. No indefinite stuck reservation.

## Independent watchers

At least two authenticated watcher identities with independent process supervision/timers inspect the same authority. One should be outside the model principal/head process tree and its cgroup. Each records durable tick receipts and checks its peer tick; the OS service manager can restart the existing owned watcher service. Do not install duplicate production daemons or invent a fallback scheduler. Watcher duties are deterministic checks, election transactions and launcher intent delivery; periodic LLM polling is unnecessary. Full host loss remains a known boundary until an independent-host quorum/store exists.

## Required acceptance cases

1. Two concurrent contenders: one winner/epoch/launch intent, loser fenced; all stale renew/assign/release attempts rejected.
2. Principal lease expires: eligible head promoted; replacement head intent recorded exactly once; live useful child work survives.
3. Desktop disappears: remote coordinator emits a scheduled check and standup outcome without desktop nudges.
4. Primary watcher stopped: standby continues tick/election, existing supervised watcher restored, no duplicate production scheduler.
5. Model busy/protected draft: no pane injection, no productive task killed; expired coordination custody can transfer safely.
6. Transport partition/unknown identity/quota: no forged ACK or unauthorized launch; authority-unreachable blocks elections; unrelated isolated tasks continue.
7. Authority restart, clock rollback and corrupt data: epochs preserved or conservative fail-closed; stale holders cannot reclaim writes.
8. Replacement crash before/after launch: reconciliation finds actual launch and does not duplicate writers; eligible alternative attempt recorded.
9. End-to-end consumer fencing at launcher/task registry boundaries, not only isolated lease tests.
10. Two actual useful model completion -> distinct semantic peer acceptance -> next-task first tool cycles with desktop/principal nudges withheld; genuine current actor evidence and READY reserve. These establish role recovery separately from the unmet 50-concurrent-worker target.

Evidence must pin implementation/reviewer IDs, source revision, exact commands/results, runtime identities, authority epoch, envelope IDs and actual acceptance scope. Do not label source-unit tests, mock failover or a README as deployed autonomy.

## Authority-store and host recovery boundary

Enroll an independent-host standby with a read-only authenticated backup, store schema version, authority identity, latest committed epoch/term and checkpoint hash. Backups never become writable just because SSH times out. For a confirmed failed authority host, recovery uses a single recorded restore intent and an externally enforced fence on the former host/authority endpoint; validate checkpoint and pending launch receipts, invalidate every old lease, advance authority generation, atomically publish one new authority endpoint, then reenroll healthy candidates and reconcile task custody before dispatch. If the old authority cannot be reliably fenced, hold shared mutations and elect nobody. Root/desktop loss does not require this procedure because authority stays remote. This emergency restore path preserves safety but is not automatic continuous host availability. A genuinely automatic multi-host version requires independent majority witnesses plus consumers rejecting old authority terms; two hosts without an external witness cannot safely self-promote on partition. No new paid infrastructure or broad credentials follow from this specification.
