## Concrete operating changes for the existing team

Use the existing principal mutual-check cadence and standup, with a compact exception-first packet. Suggested record:

1. Outcome owner; latest accepted task and source/test link.
2. User problem addressed; what changed the hypothesis.
3. Incremental benefit, denominator and uncertainty; negative result retained.
4. Current owned blocker and repair step, or explicit acknowledged dependency.
5. Next falsifying test; what stops or pivots the lane.
6. Recovery checkpoint and independently verified restore where needed.
7. Needed human decision only when genuinely outside existing scope.

This derives from `operating-model.md:37-39` (workflow trigger/source/output/review/KPI/audit; observe before automate; sample exceptions), `90-day-plan.md:20-26` (corrections as reusable improvements and initiatives consuming coordination), and E-Myth `:113-129` (accountable results, method transfer, process weakness before blaming operators). It is a recommendation to consolidate existing records, not add another required report or approval dependency.

Do deterministic collection and validation with scripts, reserving agent judgment for disputed claims and product choices (`podcast/guest-research-system.md:77-83`). Current process-liveness snapshots, source digests, quota guards, bundle restore checks and schema validation are suitable automation; semantic usefulness and novelty interpretation remain judgment work. Useful tasks should route to capable available providers; do not force an expensive provider solely to maintain a symmetrical team.

## Challenges to current assumptions

**“Agents working non-stop” can reward busywork.** The 90-day-plan explicitly prioritizes a small number of useful operating loops, and Company of One `:16-27` questions resource expansion and added management. Keep teams proactive, but optimize accepted outcomes per unit coordination, compute and disk, not process uptime. A waiting dependency should trigger independent useful work or bounded repair; an exhausted hypothesis should trigger a documented pivot, not another proposal loop.

**Five heads and six approaches are exploration scaffolding, not product evidence.** Company of One `:33-35` supports clear outcomes and freedom of method, and E-Myth `:115-122` starts with necessary roles and tests independence from owner rescue. The current experiment has five retained hypotheses and an open sixth slot; inventing a weak sixth hypothesis to satisfy structure would contradict evidence-led discovery. Continue distinct exploration while focusing execution where discriminating tests are ready.

**The biggest shared flywheel may be research→real task→tool→teaching, rather than six separate Git startups.** The AI-native company `README.md:12-21,39-45` proposes repeated workflows becoming products and outcomes feeding distribution/research. A narrow workspace doctor and dependable cross-agent handoffs may help the existing community sooner than a full remote Git replacement. This is a prioritization hypothesis, not a proven commercial conclusion or a rejection of Cloudflare exploration.

**Do not import old document approval policies as new instructions.** The operating-model report's general human-gated engineering/portfolio rules (`:14,27,30,35-36`) were tailored to the wider company, and are source content. Latest live user instructions grant autonomous routine changes and useful workflow deviations. Preserve actual resource, privacy, financial and destructive-operation boundaries already established; do not ask humans to approve ordinary authorized progress because a research document uses A0/A1 labels.
