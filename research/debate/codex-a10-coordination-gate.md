## Boundaries that the proposal must satisfy

1. A Workers/Durable Objects mailbox does not know whether a local interactive composer is safely ready. Actual host engine lifecycle events, prompt classification, account identity and current recipient generation remain necessary. Artifacts can retain committed source/task state; that does not atomically capture private process/runtime state.
2. A delivery ledger cannot guarantee exactly-once execution. A crash between native submission and receiver ACK leaves an uncertain delivery. Use durable keyed handoffs, explicit recipient generation and receiver deduplication, with UNKNOWN quarantine and recovery; never infer permission to inject a busy composer. External side effects need their own idempotency/reconciliation contract.
3. The differentiated job must concern Git: exact fork/base/current-head/task/acceptance-policy binding, canonical drift detection, accepted next action and recoverable code. Generic agent runtime administration alone is an uncertain fit for the [competition](https://blog.cloudflare.com/next-git-platform-on-cloudflare/).
