Use small disposable fixtures with declared transient disk budgets; record initial free space and refuse tests below their minimum. Keep all mutable source/output isolated; do not hardlink editable files. No installs in real user worktrees, global-cache changes or opportunistic cleanup. Larger 1/5/10/20 actual-build storage trials follow a separate budget. Immutable dependency sharing is already strong, not a new forge invention.
