Coordination of who is editing what is handled by the coordination tool, not by documents.

## 1. What this is
An entry for Cloudflare's "next Git platform" contest (deadline 14 Oct 2026; Workers and Artifacts required).
**Agent Branches** gives each coding agent its own fork and warns humans which agents' work will collide before merge.
A team of AI agents on a rented Hetzner server ran research and is building three more products (Agent Dashboard, Quota Launcher, Cross-computer Coordination). The whole run, the "experiment", is journaled publicly.
Terms: a *principal* is a top-level Claude or Codex agent that supervises but does not code; a *head* runs one product and launches workers; *Win35* is the human's own Windows laptop agent; an *ACK* means "message read", not "work done".
