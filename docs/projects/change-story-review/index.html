Change-story review

Internal use; comparative benefit unproven
Provisional idea; no final shortlist has been approved.Status as of 2026-10-03 07:07 UTC.

01 · PROBLEM

Reviewers remain responsible for quality as coding agents create more changes. A longer generated summary can increase work or anchor a reviewer on an inaccurate story.

Who: an accountable reviewer on a team that accepts AI-written changes.

02 · HYPOTHESIS

Connect a task contract, exact diff, tested behavior, impacted consumers, and evidence links in a concise review artifact. Keep transcripts optional.

03 · EVIDENCE SO FAR

LATEST3 October: the first real internal review (N=1) kept the same APPROVE verdict and confidence 80 with and without the story card. The card prompted extra checking and contained a timing error. This is descriptive internal use, not a controlled timing benefit or external demand; incumbent overlap remains.website/projects.json
